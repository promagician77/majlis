        Real in this demo

        • Server thread owns shuffle, deal, turn order, legality, scoring and settlement
        • Per-seat messages: your phone never receives another hand
        • Commit-reveal shuffle checked with WebCrypto
        • Double-entry coin ledger with idempotent settlement and no cash-out path
        • Drop / reconnect with 8 s seat grace, bot takeover, full resync
        • Delayed spectator feed, preset chat with rate limit
        • Arabic / English with right-to-left layout
        • Referee tests: npm test (13 tests) and the Stress tab

        Stand-ins to replace

        • Card rules: a Baloot-style trick game with a challenge / withdraw round, until your rulebook arrives
        • Web Worker in place of the Nakama server
        • Bots are simple heuristics
        • Coin store and subscriptions are drawn, not wired
